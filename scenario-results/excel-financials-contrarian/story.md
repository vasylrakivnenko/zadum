# A Day with the Financial Dashboard

1. Nadia the Bookkeeper signs in and opens the March 2024 summary, which was created automatically at the start of the month and shows empty totals, ready for figures.
2. Nadia uploads the file financials_Mar2024.xlsx for March; the system checks every row before saving anything.
3. Because one row lists an amount in euros, the whole file is refused and Nadia sees exactly which rows were bad — nothing is saved.
4. Nadia fixes the file to all US dollars and re-uploads; the March income lines, expense lines, and the cash balance all load, and the summary fills in: income $84,200, expenses $61,500, net profit $22,700.
5. Nadia corrects one line — Product Sales to $58,000 — and adds a Rent expense of $8,500 for the Sales department; the totals and net profit update on the spot.
6. Nadia enters the Operating Account cash balance of $142,300 as of March 31, then, with both income and expense lines present, finalizes the month.
7. Owen the Owner opens the March summary, checks the Gross Margin figure of 27% against its 30% target, and compares gross margin across the last few quarters.
8. Owen proposes a Q2 Marketing budget of $16,000 for the Sales department and approves it in the same step, so it becomes active immediately.
9. Jordan the Sales Manager signs in and reviews the Sales budget against actual Sales spending for the period.
10. Jordan tries to open the Operations department's costs, but they are hidden — a manager only sees their own area unless the owner grants more.
11. Jordan tries to change a March expense amount, but the change is refused because managers can only look, not edit.
12. Alex the Accountant opens finalized March, exports the summary to Excel for the working papers, then marks March reviewed, which locks all of March's lines against any changes.
13. Nadia later spots an error and tries to re-upload a corrected March file, but the replacement is refused because March is locked; only the Accountant can reopen it.
14. Alex reopens March, Nadia makes the fix, and Alex reviews and locks March again.
15. Owen opens the Sales department to archive it, but it still has budgets and expense lines attached, so archiving is blocked until those are reassigned elsewhere.
16. Owen opens the change history and sees every upload, edit, budget approval, and review stamped with who did it and when; none of these records can be altered or removed.
