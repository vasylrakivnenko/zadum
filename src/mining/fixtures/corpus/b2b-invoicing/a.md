# Billing platform requirements

The firm creates an invoice for each client and sends it by email.
Every invoice has line items, a total and a due date.
A client may pay an invoice online with a card.
Partial payment is allowed, and the balance remains outstanding until the invoice is settled.
When an amount is wrong the firm issues a credit note against the original invoice.
Each invoice references the purchase order supplied by the client.
Staff can see the payment history for every client.
