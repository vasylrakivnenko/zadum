== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= Q. Can I add a payment method? =
A. Just use any standard the product payment gateway.

= Q. How do withdrawals work? =
A. Right now, we offer PayPal, Bank and Skrill (pro) withdraw method for "the product". For PayPal withdraw, you can generate Mass Payment File to payout your sellers at once. Other methods are manual though.

= Q. Does the product integrate with BuddyPress? =
A. Not for the moment. We have plans to integrate with BuddyPress via an add-on in the near future.

= Q. Is the product responsive? =
A. Yes, the product is fully responsive. We take mobile first approach and it displays very well in mobile and tablet devices. We are using Twitter Bootstrap as a framework and it just works.

= Q. Does it supports variable products? =
A. The **Pro** version does! You can sell normal products, downloadable products, and variable products with your own attributes.

= Q. Can each vendor customize his store? =
A. Right now we have options for only changing the store banner page. We are working on a better customizable seller store page.

= Q. Will it be possible to take a cut or commission from the sales? =
A. Yes, that's how the product works. In every sales, you can cut a commission from a order. That's configurable from the product settings.

= Q. Can the product be used in a single seller mode? =
A. Obviously, you can use this on a single seller site to give them a nice front-end experience.

= Q. How to upgrade to the Pro version?
A. Just install and activate the PRO version without deleting the free plugin. All your data will be safe on the database and some extra features will be added. If you feel something may go wrong, please consult with our [support]( staff first.
